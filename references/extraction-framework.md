# Evidence and Interpretation Framework

Use this when adding or revising material for the Socrates skill.

## Build a Claim From Its Source

Record the author, work, edition or translation when known, location, URL, and
capture date. Inspect the actual passage: a catalog entry, search snippet, or
heading list cannot establish the argument in the work. Preserve the original
language of captured text and mark incomplete captures.

Separate three levels:

1. **Textual evidence:** what an identified passage says in its context.
2. **Interpretation:** how the passage is understood, including disagreements
   between sources or readings that could change the conclusion.
3. **Modern application:** a proposed use of a concept in circumstances the
   historical author did not address. State the analogy and where it stops.

Do not infer a person's entire worldview from one striking saying. Compare
relevant passages without imposing a fixed source count. Missing evidence is a
gap to disclose, not an invitation to pad the bibliography.

## Preserve Differences

Keep competing portraits, textual layers, and translation choices visible.
Avoid inventing a reconciliation or pretending all material associated with a
tradition belongs to its best-known figure. Distinguish an author's argument from
words assigned to a character in a dialogue.

Retain only guidance that changes a decision, evidence choice, or response.
Topic-name substitution and repeated general advice are not research depth.
Use ordinary language rather than mimicking an imagined historical voice.

## Check the Result

- Can a reader follow the supporting passage and see what it actually covers?
- Are factual claims, interpretive judgments, and modern commitments separated?
- Does the suggested method preserve the user's scope and constraints?
- Does a counterexample reveal a real limitation rather than a rhetorical trap?
- Are source rights and translation limits retained independently of the project's license?

Validate document links and source metadata, then try a realistic request that
could expose overreach. Mechanical checks do not establish historical truth or
good judgment.
